# Ondo Juvenile Correctional Home Dashboard

## 📊 Project Overview

This project presents an interactive **Power BI dashboard analyzing children recorded at the Ondo Juvenile Correctional Home**.

The analysis explores demographic characteristics, age distribution, nature of offences, referral sources, and contact-person information.

The goal was to transform raw administrative data into an interactive dashboard that makes important patterns easier to identify, interpret, and communicate.

---

## 🎯 Analytical Questions

This project seeks to answer the following questions:

1. How many children are recorded in the dataset?
2. What is the average age of the children?
3. What is the gender distribution?
4. Which offences are most frequently recorded?
5. Which age groups are most represented?
6. What are the main referral sources?
7. What does the contact-person information reveal?
8. What key patterns and areas of concern can be identified?
9. What recommendations can be made based on the findings?

## 📌 Key Findings

### 👥 Gender Distribution

The recorded population is predominantly male, with **79.3% male children** compared with **20.7% female children**.

### 🎂 Age Distribution

The average age is **14.75 years**, with children aged **13–17** representing the largest proportion of the recorded population.

### ⚖️ Nature of Offences

**Stealing** is the most frequently recorded offence by a substantial margin. Other recorded offences include wandering, rape, sexual exploitation, murder, unlawful assault, shelter, and sexual intercourse.

### 🏛️ Referral Sources

**Court referrals** account for the majority of recorded referrals, followed by parent referrals.

### 📞 Contact-Person Information

Most records indicate **No Contact Person**, highlighting a potential gap in available or documented family and guardian contact information.
---

## 💡 Insights

### 👥 Male Children Are Disproportionately Represented

The strong male majority suggests that intervention and rehabilitation programmes should consider the specific needs and circumstances of male adolescents while maintaining appropriate support for female children.

### 🎂 Adolescence Is the Critical Age Range

The concentration of children between **13 and 17 years** highlights the importance of targeted prevention, education, counselling, and rehabilitation programmes for adolescents.

### ⚖️ Stealing Requires Particular Attention

The significant difference between stealing and other recorded offences suggests that further investigation into the social, economic, family, and environmental factors associated with property-related offences may be valuable.

### 🏛️ The Court Is the Main Entry Point

The dominance of court referrals shows a strong connection between the institution and the formal justice system, creating an opportunity for improved coordination between relevant agencies.

### 📞 Contact-Person Information Is a Concern

The high number of records without an identified contact person could make family engagement, follow-up, and reintegration more difficult.
---

## 🎯 Recommendations

### 🎓 Strengthen Adolescent-Focused Intervention

Develop targeted education, counselling, life-skills, and vocational programmes for children aged 13–17.

### 🔍 Investigate the Drivers of Stealing

Conduct further assessment of the social, economic, family, and environmental factors associated with children involved in stealing.

### 👨‍👩‍👧 Strengthen Family Tracing and Engagement

Improve procedures for identifying, verifying, and documenting parents, guardians, and caregivers.

### 🤝 Improve Inter-Agency Coordination

Strengthen collaboration between the correctional home, courts, social welfare services, and relevant child-protection agencies.

### 📊 Improve Data Quality and Monitoring

Standardise the recording of contact information, referral sources, offences, and demographic information to support more reliable future analysis.
